# Project Overview
Project for my MA.
Serious game to support players in their ability to distinguish betweeen real and AI-generated media as well as their self-awareness about these decisions.

The player has to decide for different media whether they are real or AI generated.
A visual office grows depending on his trust value which reflects his decisions.
Different learning strategies are used to support the player, including direct/indirect feedback, tips, statistical insights, reflection and more.

The game logs user decisions, confidence values, interaction data, and session information.
